# Branches

## Manage branch publication without expanding Sagay operations

The approved Admin Branch Management request supersedes configuration-only email/hours rules: store these in branches; configuration is bootstrap/backfill only. Only published records enter web/mobile directories and chatbot facts. New publication/republication requires address and hours; continuously published legacy blanks may remain unchanged. Sagay's city and published status are protected; other branches never enable stock, pickup, delivery, order or reporting operations. Seed by hidden seed_key, preserve IDs and administrator edits/publication, and provide no deletion route.
